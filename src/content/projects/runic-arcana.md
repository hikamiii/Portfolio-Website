# Runic Arcana

{{ITCH_BUTTON}}

## Overview

Runic Arcana leans into readable silhouettes, bold color, and an immediately legible magical setting. It feels like the start of a larger world with its own rules and tone.

## Core Ideas

- Fantasy exploration with a strong visual hook
- Magic-forward worldbuilding
- Pixel-art environments that carry atmosphere

## Design Principles

The project stands out through clarity of style. The art direction gives it personality quickly, which is critical for a game that wants players to trust its world immediately.
