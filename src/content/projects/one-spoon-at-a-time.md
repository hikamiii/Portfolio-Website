# One Spoon at a Time

{{ITCH_BUTTON}}

## Overview

One Spoon at a Time focuses on repetition and persistence. The core joke and the core mechanic support each other, turning small movement into the entire point of play.

## Core Ideas

- Incremental progress as the main interaction
- Humor built from restraint and repetition
- A compact structure that turns a tiny action into tension

## Design Principles

The concept works because it commits fully to a narrow idea. By refusing to overcomplicate the loop, the game lets the player feel every minor gain.
